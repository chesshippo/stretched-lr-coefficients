This packet supplies a conventional mathematical proof and exact computational
evidence. It does not contain a completed Lean proof of the full finite-box
theorem. The following obligations remain for kernel verification.

1. Define finite partitions, balance, ordinary monomial coefficients, LR arrays
   and the literal integer hive lattice. Prove the article's integral coordinate
   bijection, boundedness and compatibility with stretching.
2. Formalize the precise polynomiality, saturation, multiplicity-one and
   multiplicity-two results used in the article, or prove sufficient checked
   alternatives. Published citations cannot remain unrestricted unproved axioms
   in a fully verified result.
3. Prove the explicit shortening, deletion, factorization, column, scaling,
   complement and rectangular identities. Supply the essential Horn and second
   reduction results or verify the precise finite maps consumed by the cover.
4. Prove the necessary-domain conditions and the global least-counterexample
   implication, using the article's well-founded key and target formulas.
5. Discharge E and R with a proved enumerator and local certificate checker
   applied to the supplied lists, including all indices, premises and endpoints.
6. Discharge P using a verified exact Ehrhart algorithm, complete cone/lattice
   certificates accepted by a proved checker, or enough exact integer counts
   together with a proved polynomial degree bound. Saved coefficients, hashes,
   process success and a few values do not alone establish this obligation.
7. Compose the result for every coefficient and every triple in the exact box;
   audit statement fidelity and the complete axiom census, with no admitted
   proof terms.

The repository has substantial checked foundations and reductions, but the full
finite computational closure is unfinished. The external exact Normaliz and
lrcalc implementations remain trusted in the present conventional proof.
The package provides a finite target and reproducible source data; it does not
claim that automatic translation alone completes these obligations.
