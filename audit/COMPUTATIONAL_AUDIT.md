# Independent computational audit

The complete unmodified report is
[computational_audit/COMPUTATIONAL_AUDIT.md](computational_audit/COMPUTATIONAL_AUDIT.md).
Its structured results and original source program are in the same directory.
The final synthesis is [FINAL_AUDIT.md](FINAL_AUDIT.md); the later full raw
reproduction is documented separately in [FRESH_EVIDENCE.md](FRESH_EVIDENCE.md).

The computational audit checked every actual frozen certificate with strict
partition, parameter, target, scale and coverage requirements. It also records
deliberately malformed inputs accepted by portions of the historical generic
checker. The publication erratum distinguishes those general parser weaknesses
from the validity of every actual certificate used in dependency R.
