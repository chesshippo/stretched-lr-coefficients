\vspace{0.55cm}
For every balanced partition triple $(\lambda,\mu,\nu)$ with all lengths at
most seven and all sizes at most thirty, every ordinary monomial coefficient
of its stretched Littlewood--Richardson polynomial is nonnegative.
\[
 [t^i]P^{\lambda}_{\mu\nu}(t)\ge0\qquad(i\ge0).
\]
The next page gives publication clarifications; the subsequent 19 pages
are the unchanged reviewed manuscript. The full
computational supplement, source programs, exact records, and a reproducible
audit accompany this release. The proof relies on the identified exact
Normaliz and lrcalc computations; full Lean verification remains unfinished.
Two internal AI referee sessions passed the full argument. External
mathematical acceptance and Epoch endorsement are not asserted.
