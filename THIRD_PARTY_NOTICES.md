# Third-party credit and dependencies

The project licenses cover original project contributions only. No dependency
binaries, installed Python packages, unrelated research papers, provider
conversations, API credentials or repository history are bundled.

| Dependency/source | Credit and status |
| --- | --- |
| LR Calculator and Python lrcalc 2.1 | Anders Skovsted Buch and upstream contributors. Python bindings GPL-3.0-or-later; installed separately. https://pypi.org/project/lrcalc/ and https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~asbuch/lrcalc/ |
| Normaliz 3.11.1 | The Normaliz team and contributors. Exact Ehrhart engine; installed separately for fresh regeneration. Its own distribution license governs. https://github.com/Normaliz/Normaliz/tree/v3.11.1 |

| Epoch problem statement | Epoch AI and cited problem sources. Source-derived statement material in archived problem.md/spec.json is credited to https://epoch.ai/frontiermath/open-problems/stretched-lr-coefficients and excluded from any claim of original authorship |
| Published mathematical results | Original authors as cited in the paper bibliography. These inputs retain their credit; the release claims no exclusive authorship of them |
| GNU and Creative Commons license texts | Their respective organizations. Included as standard unmodified license documents under the permissions supplied with those documents |

Engine output banners and upstream attribution inside the archival evidence
are retained exactly. Any third-party quotation or source-derived material
remains subject to its existing rights; project-level licenses do not supersede
those rights. Original audit records retain historical local paths as provenance.

Epoch-derived statement material is adapted from the linked Epoch AI page
under CC-BY-4.0; the archived specification adds formal definitions and
conventions. See ARCHIVAL_ERRATA.md for the corrected attribution of the
separately cited Thawinrak paper. No original authorship of Epoch's prompt
or prior mathematical results is claimed.

The PDFs embed subsets of Computer Modern, AMS and Latin Modern fonts.
Their designers and upstream licenses retain their credit and terms.
See https://ctan.org/pkg/amsfonts and https://ctan.org/pkg/lm.
No standalone font package or engine binary is distributed.
