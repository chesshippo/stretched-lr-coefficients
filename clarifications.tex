\medskip
Read this page together with the unchanged v010 manuscript that follows.
These corrections make implicit conventions explicit and correct descriptions
and attribution. They change no coefficient, reduction formula, residual set,
or finite-box conclusion.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Lemma 6: complement width.} The rectangle width is an integer
$M\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$ with $M\ge\alpha_1$. Its proof counts columns;
all applications use integer partition parts as widths.
\item \textbf{Theorem 7: ambient rank.} Take an integer $n\ge1$ at least as
large as the lengths of the three partitions, so $h=\lambda_n$ is defined.
The all-empty input separately has polynomial $P=1$. Every nonempty
least-witness application has positive rank.
\item \textbf{Certificate validation description.} The sentence in
the subsection ``R: local certificates and complete coverage'' saying that
malformed partitions and invalid parameters are
rejected overstates the original parser's general behavior. It removes
internal zero parts and treats an unrecognized inner selector as $\nu$.
The independent exhaustive strict audit instead validates the original raw
frozen records before normalization. Every actual edge has valid partition
order, selector and integer parameters, and satisfies its rule and target
conditions. The supplied certificates therefore meet the validity requirements
of dependency R. See the accompanying computational audit and source.
\item \textbf{Archived reference attribution.} The frozen specification's
reference to arXiv:2211.06810 should credit \textbf{Warut Thawinrak}, correcting
the recorded name ``R. Wong.'' The title is \emph{A Short Proof for the
Polynomiality of the Stretched Littlewood--Richardson Coefficients}
(2022, revised 2024), \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.06810}.
This error is absent from the current manuscript's bibliography.
\end{enumerate}
The exact archival paths, TeX line references and hashes are listed in
\texttt{ARCHIVAL\_ERRATA.md}. Frozen manuscript and evidence bytes are retained
for reproducibility. The new audit records and this addendum are separately
identified; they do not rewrite historical referee reports.
